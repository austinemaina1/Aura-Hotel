Welcome to Aura Hotel Nairobi

Located in the heart of Nairobi, Aura Hotel offers a perfect blend of comfort, elegance, and authentic Kenyan hospitality. Whether you are visiting for business, leisure, or a family getaway, our modern accommodations, exceptional dining, and personalized service ensure an unforgettable stay.

Conveniently situated near Nairobi's business districts, shopping centers, Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA), and popular attractions such as Nairobi National Park, Aura Hotel provides the perfect environment for both relaxation and productivity.
