Our Journey

A Leading Hospitality Destination

Today, Aura Hotel Nairobi stands as a trusted destination for travelers, business professionals, and families seeking comfort, luxury, and exceptional service. Our modern facilities, elegant accommodations, and dedicated team continue to deliver memorable experiences to guests from Kenya and around the world.

With premium rooms, fine dining, conference facilities, recreational amenities, and personalized hospitality, Aura Hotel has become a preferred choice for both business and leisure travelers.

Expansion & Modernization

As guest expectations evolved, Aura Hotel invested in modern infrastructure, upgraded guest rooms, enhanced conference facilities, and introduced new dining experiences. These improvements strengthened our commitment to providing world-class hospitality while maintaining a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

The Beginning of Aura Hotel

Aura Hotel was established with a vision of creating a hospitality destination where comfort, elegance, and personalized service come together. From its earliest days, the hotel focused on delivering exceptional guest experiences and building lasting relationships with visitors from across Kenya and beyond.
